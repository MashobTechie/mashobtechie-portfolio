# MashobTechie Portfolio — Design Brief

> Verbatim brief as given on 2026-08-16 (session `436bb178-733e-4335-b751-ce33cb719ef4`).
> Closing instruction: build with **Next.js + Tailwind CSS**; further design references to follow.

Design a premium personal freelance portfolio website for **MashobTechie**, a young full-stack software engineer and SaaS builder who helps businesses launch, grow, and sell online.

This is NOT a typical developer portfolio and should NOT look like a dark-mode coding/AI website.

The visual direction should feel like a **premium modern digital studio / product consultancy**: clean, confident, approachable, technically credible, business-focused, and conversion-oriented.

## BRAND POSITIONING

Primary positioning:

**"I build websites & SaaS that grow businesses."**

Supporting message:

**"Helping businesses launch, grow & sell online."**

The website should communicate that MashobTechie does more than write code. He helps businesses turn ideas, products, and opportunities into polished digital experiences.

Target clients:

* Small and growing businesses
* E-commerce brands
* Creators and personal brands
* Startups and founders
* Service businesses
* Companies that need a website, web application, SaaS MVP, dashboard, or digital product

The visitor should immediately understand:

1. What MashobTechie does
2. Who he helps
3. What he has built
4. Why they should trust him
5. How to start a project

## BRAND PERSONALITY

Premium + modern + approachable + technically credible.

Avoid:

* Developer cliché aesthetics
* Dark hacker themes
* Neon green
* Purple AI gradients
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Terminal/code backgrounds
* Excessive animations
* Cyberpunk styling
* Overly futuristic visuals
* Generic template appearance

The design should feel closer to a **high-end digital product studio** than a software engineer résumé.

Use #2563EB strategically for:

* Primary CTA buttons
* Important links
* Small highlights
* Hover states
* Active navigation states
* Small visual accents

Do NOT make the entire website blue.

The dominant visual experience should be warm off-white, white, and deep navy with restrained blue accents.

## TYPOGRAPHY

Use **Geist** as the primary typeface if available.

Use **Inter** as the fallback.

Typography should be:

* Large
* Clean
* Highly readable
* Modern
* Strong hierarchy
* Generous line height
* Professional

Use large editorial-style headings rather than cramped developer-dashboard typography.

## USE MY PHOTO

Use the uploaded portrait photo of MashobTechie as the primary personal visual asset.

Do NOT dramatically alter the person's appearance.

Use the photo naturally and professionally.

Possible treatment:

* Large portrait beside the hero text
* Cropped editorial portrait
* Subtle rounded rectangle or very subtle radius
* Clean white/off-white surrounding space

Do NOT use a circular developer-avatar treatment as the primary hero design.

The photo should make the website feel personal and trustworthy rather than like an anonymous agency.

## NAVIGATION

Create a minimal sticky navigation.

Left:
**MashobTechie**

Right:

* Work
* Services
* About
* Contact

Primary CTA:
**Start a Project**

Keep navigation extremely clean.

On mobile, use a compact hamburger menu.

Main headline:

**I build websites & SaaS that grow businesses.**

Supporting copy:

**Helping businesses launch, grow & sell online with modern websites and web applications built for real customers—not just good-looking demos.**

Primary CTA:

Secondary CTA:

**View My Work**

Include a small credibility line such as:

**Websites • SaaS • E-commerce • Web Apps**

Place my uploaded portrait prominently but elegantly within the hero.

The hero should feel premium, spacious, and confident.

Do not overcrowd it.

## TRUST / SOCIAL PROOF STRIP

Immediately below the hero, create a subtle trust section.

Possible text:

**Built for businesses, startups & ambitious founders.**

Then show small proof indicators such as:

* SaaS products shipped
* E-commerce experiences
* Real client projects
* Startup experience
* Full-stack development

Do not fabricate exact statistics.

Where numerical proof is not explicitly available, use qualitative statements rather than invented numbers.

## CASE STUDY STYLE

When a project is opened, use a premium case-study layout.

Structure:

1. Project overview
2. Business problem
3. Solution
4. My role
5. Key features
6. Product screenshots
7. Technology
8. Outcome / impact where verified
9. CTA

Focus on business outcomes rather than listing code libraries.

For example, instead of:

"Built with Next.js, TypeScript and Prisma."

Prefer:

"Built a scalable e-commerce experience with product management, checkout and payment integration."

Technology can still appear lower in the case study.

## SERVICES

Create a section titled:

**What I Build**

Use 4–5 clean cards.

### Business Websites

Modern websites designed to turn visitors into customers.

### E-commerce

Online stores with product management, checkout and payment integrations.

### SaaS & MVPs

Turn an idea into a functional product customers can actually use.

### Web Applications

Dashboards, portals, internal tools and custom business applications.

### Product Improvements

## PROCESS

Create a section:

**From idea to launch.**

Show a simple 4-step process:

01 — Understand
Understand the business, users and goals.

02 — Design
Plan the experience and create the interface.

03 — Build
Develop and integrate the product.

04 — Launch
Deploy, test and hand over a production-ready product.

Use a clean horizontal layout on desktop and vertical layout on mobile.

## DESIGN SYSTEM

Use:

* Large whitespace
* Strong visual hierarchy
* 12-column desktop grid
* Maximum content width around 1200–1280px
* Generous section spacing
* 8px spacing system
* Subtle 1px borders
* Very subtle shadows
* Moderate corner radius
* Large high-quality screenshots
* Editorial-style typography
* Clean cards
* Smooth but restrained transitions

Do not over-round everything.

Avoid excessive pill-shaped UI.

Avoid excessive gradients.

Avoid excessive shadows.

## BUTTON STYLE

Primary buttons:

* Blue #2563EB background
* White text
* Medium radius
* Strong but clean typography

Secondary buttons:

* Transparent / white background
* Navy text
* Thin border

Use arrows sparingly for directional CTAs.

## RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Design desktop first but make the system fully responsive.

Mobile should feel intentionally designed, not like a collapsed desktop layout.

Prioritize:

* Fast scanning
* Large readable typography
* Touch-friendly buttons
* Strong project screenshots
* Clear CTA
* Simple navigation

## OVERALL CREATIVE DIRECTION

Imagine the website as the intersection of:

**Premium digital agency + modern SaaS product studio + personal founder brand.**

It should make a potential client think:

**"This person understands business, design and technology—and I can trust him to build this."**

The website should NOT make them think:

**"This is just another developer portfolio."**

Create the complete responsive website UI including:

* Homepage
* Portfolio/work section
* Project case-study template
* Services
* About
* Contact
* Navigation
* Footer
* Mobile layouts

Prioritize conversion, credibility, visual quality and clarity over technical complexity.
